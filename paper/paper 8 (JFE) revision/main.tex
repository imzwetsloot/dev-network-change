\begin{abstract}

Online social networks play an important role in financial markets and facilitate information sharing among investors. In the literature either theoretical models exist for the effect of individual social network users on financial markets or aggregated empirical models are tested on small networks, whereas real social networks like Reddit are large scale. We study this relationship empirically with a hand collected network dataset from WallStreetBets, a subreddit, comprising of 3.8 million posts from 502,650 users via a modelling approach we term the Interpretable Social Network and Market Behaviour modelling approach (ISMB); an approach which captures heterogeneous individual user behaviour and links this through innovative change point modelling to the stock market. We find that 1) especially followers of influential users impact market behaviour, 2) network changes occur well before the market frenzy, 3) changes in users behaviour explain the market movements and improve modelling performance.
